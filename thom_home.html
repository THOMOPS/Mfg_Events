Why THOM

Cash Context is king.

I spent 15 years in manufacturing: med device, electronics and aerospace, leading engineering and business teams and building cultures that perform. Some of the most regulated industries there are, where "that's not how we do it here" is the default answer.

I helped take a line of business from zero to multiple millions by doing the exact things everyone said were impossible. Regulation was never the real constraint. The legacy way of thinking was.

Meanwhile, the resources meant to help manufacturing were stuck in the past. Spreadsheets. PowerPoints. Anything useful locked behind expensive consultants and enterprise systems. No soul, and nothing that said this is one of the most innovative industries on earth.

Then it clicked: cash is no longer king. Context is. The companies that win capture what they know and put it to work.

THOM is different, and that's on purpose. We think forward, and our default is to do, not to wait around for the perfect agreement. We don't do soft, and we don't do long-ass PowerPoints nobody reads.
